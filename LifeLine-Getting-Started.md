## 1. Activate your owner workspace

Open LifeLine in a normal browser tab. Select **Church owner**, then **Sign in securely**. Sign in with the Manus account you want to use as the church owner.

Download the [private one-time owner activation key](/home/ubuntu/churchconnect-private/owner-activation-key.txt). Open the downloaded text file and copy its key. When LifeLine returns from sign-in, paste the key into **Owner activation key** and select **Activate owner workspace**. The account you signed in with becomes the owner. The key works once and expires December 3, 2026 at 11:48 UTC, or 6:48 AM Eastern time.

Keep this key private. It is not a tester invitation, and it must not be shared with members. For later visits, sign in with the same owner account; you do not need the key again. Your real owner sign-in and activation must be completed by you; no owner identity has been claimed on your behalf.

## 2. Prepare the bulletin

In **Weekly bulletin**, edit the date, sermon title, scripture, speaker, announcements and events. Sunday worship is **11:00 AM**. The church address is **105 Church Street, Totowa, NJ 07512**. The website and real church logo are already displayed.

Leave giving and livestream URLs empty until you have verified the actual church destinations. Enter only complete HTTPS links. LifeLine does not process donations or send email.

Choose **Save draft**, then **Preview**. After checking the saved version, choose **Publish saved draft** and confirm. This makes the saved content visible to admitted testers; it does not require another website deployment. Draft changes do not appear to testers until you publish the saved draft.

## 3. Invite a small testing group

Open **Pilot access**, give the invitation a label, and create it. Copy the private invitation immediately and share it yourself with authorized testers. Testers open it without creating an account or purchasing an AI subscription. Each invitation expires after seven days and can be revoked.

The deployed website is reachable, but bulletin APIs still require the owner or a valid invitation. Publishing did not remove the application’s admission gate. This remains a restricted sample-data pilot, not an unrestricted congregational rollout. The pilot continues to label weekly bulletin content as sample data.

## 4. Use the published QR code

Open **Sharing & QR code** while signed in on the live LifeLine site. Copy the published congregation address or download its QR. Do not reuse the earlier Preview QR: it points to the development environment.

The QR contains no invitation secret. A tester must first redeem their private invitation in the same browser. A phone scanner that opens another browser may require admission again. Do not distribute an invitation-bearing URL or the owner activation key as a general QR.

## What members can do

Admitted members can read the published bulletin, print it, copy the newsletter and take sermon notes. Notes stay in that browser only; they are not sent to church staff, not synced, and can be lost when browser data is cleared.

The next essential step is **your owner sign-in and activation**, followed by one saved draft, preview and bulletin publication. Publishing the website does not automatically claim your account, remove sample labels, add giving/streaming links or make the member bulletin unrestricted.
