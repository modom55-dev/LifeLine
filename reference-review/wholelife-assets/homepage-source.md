KID’S KINGDOM · OUR YOUTH CHURCH

WHERE THE NEXT
GENERATION BELONGS.

Kid’s Kingdom is our youth church for ages 3 to 17. It’s a safe, fun, and Christ-centered space where children and teens grow in faith through age-appropriate worship, teaching, and community. From our youngest learners to our teens, every child is known, loved, and encouraged to discover their God-given purpose.

Kid’s Kingdom meets during Sunday service, so parents can worship while their children learn on their level. Come as you are — there’s a place here for your whole family.
