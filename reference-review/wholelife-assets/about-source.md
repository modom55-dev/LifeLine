SENIOR PASTOR

PASTOR MICHAEL
H. ODOM, SR.
AUTHOR · PASTOR · EDUCATOR
COMMUNITY LEADER
01
CALLING & FORMATION

Pastor Michael H. Odom, Sr. is an author, pastor, certified counselor, educator, speaker, podcast host, and community leader committed to helping people heal, lead, and live a better story.

02
PASTORAL LEADERSHIP

For more than 35 years, he has served through ministry, leadership development, counseling, teaching, workforce preparation, youth engagement, and community transformation. Since 2007, he has served as Senior Pastor of WholeLife Community Church in Totowa, New Jersey, where he leads with a heart for spiritual growth, healthy families, practical service, and lasting community impact.

03
COMMUNITY IMPACT

Born in Washington, D.C. and raised in Newark, New Jersey, Pastor Odom's work is rooted in a simple conviction: where a person has been does not have to determine where they are going. Through faith, healing, truth, and practical tools, people can reclaim their identity and build a stronger future.

04
EDUCATION & SERVICE

He is the author of The Becoming of You, The Ghetto Ghost, and The Ghetto Ghost Workbook. His writing speaks to faith, identity, trauma, healing, purpose, leadership, and the ongoing work of personal transformation.

05
MINISTRY LEGACY

His work also includes The Psychological Effects of Salvation: The Transformative Work of the Holy Spirit, a theological and scientific exploration of what happens within a person when salvation takes root. Drawing from Scripture, neuroscience, and psychology, Pastor Odom examines how the Holy Spirit renews the mind, reshapes identity, heals wounds from the past, and produces lasting change. Created for adult readers, ministry leaders, discipleship settings, and small groups, the book is part of The Psychological Effects of Salvation Set.

06
FAMILY & LIFE

The set also includes The Salvation Guided Reflection Journal: A Guided Companion to The Psychological Effects of Salvation. This journal invites readers to slow down, reflect on the Spirit's work within them, and record the evidence of transformation unfolding through personal devotion or group study.

07
WHOLELIFE TODAY

Pastor Michael H. Odom, Sr. is also the author of Listening to Lead: The Power of Active Listening in Transformational Leadership, a guide that explores listening as a foundational leadership skill. Written for leaders, managers, mentors, pastors, and anyone seeking greater influence, it teaches readers to hear beyond words—to recognize the needs, concerns, and potential in the people they serve.

08
LIFE & MINISTRY

Alongside his wife, Pastor Pamela Y. Odom, Pastor Odom is co-authoring Hearts in Harmony: Building Love Through Communication, Healing, and Connection, coming in 2027. This transformative relationship guide is designed to help couples, families, and relationship counselors build stronger bonds through authentic communication, healing past wounds, and deeper connection.

09
LIFE & MINISTRY

Since 2020, Pastor Odom has hosted Morning Manna, a podcast and devotional platform providing biblical lessons for practical living. Through thoughtful and relatable teaching, he helps listeners connect Scripture to relationships, decisions, leadership, healing, purpose, and everyday growth.

10
LIFE & MINISTRY

Pastor Odom is also the creator of LABS—Living A Better Story—through The O Factor. As a consultant, instructor, and coach, he partners with agencies and organizations to deliver customized programs that help individuals build resilience, strengthen life skills, set meaningful goals, and take ownership of their future. LABS has served justice-impacted individuals, women and mothers, youth and young adults, entrepreneurs, and alternative-education students through partnerships with Reimagining Justice, Renew Life Center, ECHO NJ, Partners Worldwide, and the Newark Board of Education Alternative Education Program.

11
LIFE & MINISTRY

A Certified Christian Counselor, SDI Certified facilitator, and John Maxwell Certified leadership trainer, Pastor Odom is known for teaching that connects biblical truth, emotional wellness, lived experience, and practical action.

12
LIFE & MINISTRY

Whether preaching, counseling, writing, hosting Morning Manna, training leaders, or mentoring the next generation, Pastor Odom carries a message of grace, responsibility, and possibility: Your past may explain parts of your story, but it does not have to write the ending.

PASTOR PAMELA
Y. ODOM
AUTHOR · EDUCATOR
WELLNESS ADVOCATE
01
CALLING & FORMATION

Pastor Pamela Y. Odom is an author, executive pastor, wellness advocate, educator, and entrepreneur committed to helping people heal, grow, and live whole.

02
PASTORAL LEADERSHIP

She serves as Executive Pastor of WholeLife Community Church alongside her husband, Pastor Michael H. Odom, Sr. Through ministry, mentorship, teaching, and community service, Pastor Pamela brings wisdom, compassion, and practical encouragement to individuals and families seeking stronger faith, healthier relationships, and renewed purpose.

03
COMMUNITY IMPACT

Pastor Pamela is the author of The Scent of a Woman and its accompanying journal, a reflective work created to encourage women to rediscover their value, embrace their identity, and honor the fragrance of purpose within their lives. She is also the author of Detox, a wellness-focused book that reflects her heart for helping others make intentional choices for their physical, emotional, and spiritual health.

04
EDUCATION & SERVICE

As a Certified Health Coach through the Dr. Sears Wellness Institute, Pastor Pamela has developed a passionate voice in the area of holistic wellness. Her own journey with fibromyalgia has strengthened her compassion for those navigating health challenges and inspired her to promote practical pathways toward healthier living. Her approach includes whole foods, juicing, natural wellness practices, education, and encouragement that meets people where they are.

05
MINISTRY LEGACY

Pastor Pamela is the owner and operator of Whole New Detox Spa, a wellness space designed to support restoration, renewal, and healthier lifestyle choices. She is also the owner and operator of WholeLife Christian Academy, founded in 2001, where she has helped shape an environment committed to faith, learning, character, and the development of children and families.

06
FAMILY & LIFE

She is a co-founder of Hearts in Harmony, a marriage and relationship ministry that equips couples to build stronger communication, deeper connection, and healthier foundations. Pastor Pamela is also a proud wife, mother, grandmother, and Charter Member of The Links, Incorporated, Passaic County Chapter.

07
WHOLELIFE TODAY

Her life and work carry one clear message: healing is possible, growth is still available, and every person has the ability to live a better, healthier, more purposeful story.
